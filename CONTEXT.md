# Nyingarn Workspace

The workspace where Australian Indigenous language manuscripts are digitised, transcribed and prepared for publication to the repository.

## Language

### Content

**Item**:
A single manuscript being digitised. It holds the images and transcriptions for that manuscript.

**Collection**:
A grouping of items and other collections. It carries shared metadata for the things it contains.

**Sub-collection**:
A collection that is a member of another collection. The relationship is symmetric: each side lists the other.

**Member**:
An item or sub-collection contained in a collection. Never a person.
_Avoid_: child, entry, and any use of "member" to mean a user

### People and access

**User**:
A person with an account in the workspace.
_Avoid_: member, collaborator, account

**Access**:
The relationship between a user and a specific item or collection that lets them work on it. Access to a collection does not imply access to its members.
_Avoid_: permission, ownership, membership

**Invite**:
The act of granting a user access to an item or collection, done by a user who already has access to it.
_Avoid_: attach, share, add

**Administrator**:
A user who has access to every item and collection without being invited.
_Avoid_: admin, superuser
